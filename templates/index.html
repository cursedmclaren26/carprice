Section 01

1. Project Overview & Leak-Free Optimization

The Car Price Predictor combines a 2026-calibrated Indian resale depreciation engine with a leak-free scikit-learn Pipeline tuned via 5-fold GridSearchCV on 6,935 cleaned CarDekho listings (5,548 train / 1,387 untouched test).

Three regression families were systematically tuned over 9 optimal reduced features (after correlation-driven pruning from 12 candidate features):

SVM / SVR (Champion ★) RBF kernel with C=10, gamma='auto' and log-target scaling inside Pipeline. Hits 84.45% 5-Fold CV R² and 91.14% Untouched Test R² (lowest gap: 3.13%).
Random Forest Regressor Bagged 500-tree ensemble with max_features='sqrt'. Achieves 83.12% 5-Fold CV R² and 92.31% Test R² (MAE ₹84,599).
K-Nearest Neighbors (KNN) Distance-weighted Euclidean similarity (k=7) over scaled specs & one-hot segments. Scores 82.85% 5-Fold CV R² and 89.53% Test R².

Section 05

5. Dual-Engine Valuation & Live Render Deployment

In production, CarValuator blends the tuned 9-feature SVR (RBF) market model with the 2026-calibrated depreciation curve (RETENTION_LEVEL = 1.36) using bounded relative adjustment (clip(ML / Dep, 0.72, 1.45), weight w=0.30 for known models and w=0.10 for unseen launches like the 2025 Kia Syros or 2026 Kia Clavis).

Final Price = P_depreciation × (1 − w + w × clip(P_SVR / P_depreciation, 0.72, 1.45))
KmMultiplier = 1.0 − 0.15 × tanh((km / benchmark_km − 1.0) × 0.75)
